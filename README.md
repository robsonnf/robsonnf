## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm an **IT Professional and Systems Development Instructor at SENAC RS (SENAC TECH)**. My background brings together enterprise IT infrastructure, L2/L3 technical support, endpoint management, cloud, automation, and software development.

Today, I combine industry experience with teaching, helping students build practical and theoretical knowledge in **systems development, mobile applications, graphical interfaces, full-stack concepts, and software projects**.

- 🌱 **Current learning:** cloud, DevOps, infrastructure as code, and observability.
- ⚡ **What drives me:** solving complex problems, automating repetitive work, documenting solutions, and sharing knowledge.
